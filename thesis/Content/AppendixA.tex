
\chapter{Cluster and Game Definitions}

\section{Cluster Descriptions}
\label{app:clusters}

\begin{itemize}
    \item \textbf{Law, Justice \& Public Policy}: Focuses on how laws, institutions, and public systems shape society, training students to analyze policy, governance, and social justice impacts.
    \item \textbf{Creative Arts \& Media}: Centers on creative expression and storytelling across visual, performing, and digital media, building craft skills and portfolios.
    \item \textbf{Business \& Management}: Prepares students to run organizations through finance, marketing, operations, and leadership with practical, decision-focused skills.
    \item \textbf{Engineering \& Technology}: Applies math and science to design, build, and optimize systems, products, and infrastructure.
    \item \textbf{Health \& Behavioral Sciences}: Explores human health, behavior, and wellbeing with a blend of biological, psychological, and applied clinical practice.
    \item \textbf{Sciences \& Mathematics}: Builds rigorous quantitative and scientific foundations for modeling, experimentation, and data-driven discovery.
    \item \textbf{Education \& Teaching}: Trains future educators to design instruction, support learners, and manage classrooms across age levels.
    \item \textbf{Humanities \& Cultural Studies}: Examines culture, history, language, and ideas to develop critical thinking and communication skills.
\end{itemize}

\section{Game Prompts}
\label{app:games}

\subsection{Game 1: Fact or Fiction Statements}

Each statement corresponds to one cluster. Students mark each as true or false for themselves; any cluster they reject is filtered out.

\begin{itemize}
    \item ``I want to participate in our systems of power, law, and policy'' (Law, Justice \& Public Policy)
    \item ``I enjoy expressing myself through creative mediums'' (Creative Arts \& Media)
    \item ``I could see myself working in a corporate setting'' (Business \& Management)
    \item ``I want to build and design new things'' (Engineering \& Technology)
    \item ``I want to understand how people behave and function'' (Health \& Behavioral Sciences)
    \item ``I'm fascinated by how the natural and physical world works'' (Sciences \& Mathematics)
    \item ``I want to help guide the next generation'' (Education \& Teaching)
    \item ``I love analyzing society and culture'' (Humanities \& Cultural Studies)
\end{itemize}

\subsection{Game 2: This or That Pairs}

Each pair captures a dimension of academic orientation. Students select one option from each.

\begin{itemize}
    \item Subjective vs. Objective
    \item Theoretical vs. Practical
    \item People vs. Systems
    \item Details vs. Big-picture
    \item Data-driven vs. Creative
    \item Response vs. Prevention
    \item Analyzing media vs. Creating media
    \item Regulate vs. Innovate
    \item Learning vs. Teaching
\end{itemize}

\subsection{Game 3: Select 3 Keywords}

Students see eight keywords drawn from each of their matched clusters and select their top three. The keyword sets by cluster are:

\begin{itemize}
    \item \textbf{Law, Justice \& Public Policy:} justice, debate, analysis, policy, collaboration, leadership, advocacy, abroad
    \item \textbf{Creative Arts \& Media:} music, design, writing, movies, art, performance, drawing, digital
    \item \textbf{Business \& Management:} finance, leadership, numbers, customers, organization, sales, hospitality, advertising
    \item \textbf{Engineering \& Technology:} buildings, medical, programming, design, hands-on, math, physics, systems
    \item \textbf{Health \& Behavioral Sciences:} behavior, chemistry, physics, sports, medicine, counseling, care, health
    \item \textbf{Sciences \& Mathematics:} math, biology, chemistry, physics, nature, experiments, research, analysis
    \item \textbf{Education \& Teaching:} students, youth, arts, sports, growth, mentoring, curriculum, cultures
    \item \textbf{Humanities \& Cultural Studies:} history, culture, language, ethics, writing, diversity, analysis, research
\end{itemize}
